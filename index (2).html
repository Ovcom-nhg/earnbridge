How it works

Four clear steps. No guesswork.

EarnBridge rewards genuine referrals—not deposits, recruitment chains or promises of automatic income.

STEP 01

Create an account

Register with accurate details and confirm your account.

STEP 02

Share your invite

Send your personal link to friends who are interested in the offer.

STEP 03

Referral is verified

The new member completes the published qualifying steps.

STEP 04

Reward is credited

Approved rewards appear in your wallet, ready under the payout rules.
